Reefill Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Reefill is a hydration tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Reefill stores, how it is used, and what choices users have.

Summary

Reefill is designed to work without an account. In the current version, Reefill does not operate its own backend server and does not upload your hydration history to Reefill-owned servers.

Information Stored On Your Device

Reefill may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Water entries, including amounts logged and the time they were added.
  • Onboarding answers such as name, age, height, weight, activity level, hydration goal, and estimated current water intake.
  • Daily hydration goal, XP, streaks, achievements, unlocked companions, equipped companion, reminder settings, and app preferences.

This information is used to calculate hydration goals, show progress, keep history, manage reminders, and personalize the app experience.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Reefill schedules local notifications on your device. These may include hydration reminders and streak-protection reminders. You can turn reminders off in Reefill or in iPhone Settings.

Purchases and Paywalls

Reefill may use Apple in-app purchases and Superwall to present paywalls, manage purchase flows, and understand paywall performance. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. Superwall may process technical and purchase-related information needed to provide its service, subject to Superwall's own privacy practices.

Data Sharing

Reefill does not sell your personal data. Reefill does not require an account in the current version and does not upload your hydration history to Reefill-owned servers.

Data Retention

Your Reefill data stays on your device until you delete it, reset the app data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may remove local data depending on iOS behavior and backup settings.

Security

Reefill uses iOS system storage and platform protections for local app data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, but Reefill is designed to minimize data collection and avoid unnecessary server-side storage.

Health Disclaimer

Reefill provides general hydration tracking and habit-building guidance. It is not medical advice. Hydration needs vary. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, take medications that affect hydration, or have physician-directed fluid limits, follow professional medical guidance.

Children

Reefill is not intended for children where parental consent is required by law, and Reefill does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Reefill changes. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
